### About me

- **Now:** M.S. Nanoscience at the Joint School of Nanoscience and Nanoengineering, UNC Greensboro (May 2027). My research is on microbial adhesion to surfaces.
- **Before:** registered nurse in Ghana, in orthopaedic and medical-surgical care. Infection prevention at the bedside is what pulled me into the lab.
- **Next:** antimicrobial resistance in water, and turning lab measurements into public-health decisions.
- **Recent work:** second author on a methods paper for a rapid microbe and surface adhesion assay, submitted to *Current Protocols*.
